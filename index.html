Singapore Soundscape Report

Crowdsourcing how Singapore's walking environments sound and feel.

Singapore's urban environments are experienced not only visually, but acoustically. This project invites members of the community to document how different walking environments sound and how those environments are perceived.

Participants contribute location-based observations of acoustic comfort, Pleasantness, Eventfulness, dominant sound sources and environmental context. Together, these reports build a community-generated map of Singapore's everyday soundscape experience.
