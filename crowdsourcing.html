About Participate Response Map

Contribute a Soundscape

Help us understand how Singapore's walking environments sound and feel. Participation takes approximately 2–3 minutes.

What Are We Collecting?

Each contribution records information about a specific soundscape location in Singapore. Participants will be asked to provide:

  • Location and place name
  • Date and time of observation
  • Overall sound comfort rating
  • Pleasantness rating
  • Eventfulness rating
  • Sounds heard and dominant sound source
  • Type of urban environment
  • A brief soundscape observation
  • Optional sound recording or photograph

How to Participate

01

Choose your location

Find a walking or public environment in Singapore and remain at the location while completing the survey.

02

Listen for one minute

Pay attention to the overall acoustic environment. Consider traffic, people, transport, mechanical sounds, nature and other sounds around you.

03

Rate your experience

Report your overall sound comfort, Pleasantness and Eventfulness, and identify the dominant sounds and type of environment.

04

Add supporting media

You may optionally upload a short sound recording or photograph. Please avoid intentionally recording identifiable private conversations or personal information.

05

Submit your report

Review your responses before submitting your report. Your contribution will help build the community soundscape map.

Before You Submit

Please do not intentionally record identifiable private conversations or personal information. Sound recordings and photographs are optional. Submitted observations may be displayed on the public community soundscape map for this academic project.
